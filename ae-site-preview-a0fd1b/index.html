Services

What we do

Every job is priced on its own, after we've seen it. Send a photo, or have us stop by for the bigger ones.

Mulch & garden beds

Mulch picked up or delivered, then spread. We've done 3 yards and we've done 25. Beds weeded first if they need it.

Hedges & weeding

Hedges trimmed, beds and stone walls weeded, brush cut back and cleared.

Leaf removal

Fall leaf cleanups. Leaves blown, gathered and cleared off the lawn.

Moving & hauling

House moves, furniture, logs and railroad ties. Junk and shed cleanouts too.

Power washing

Decks and fences washed down. We can stain or oil the wood afterward.

Boat detailing

Wash, compound, buff and wax, plus bottom paint before launch.

Stonework & small builds

Cobblestone bed edging set in concrete, stair rails and small carpentry jobs.

Snow plowing

Driveways plowed per storm. Ask about a spot on this winter's list.

Also on the list so far: painting and staining, floor demo and lawn mowing. If your job isn't here, ask. The name is Anything and Everything.
