A family practice, two generations of care

Bruno Chiropractic has served North Providence since 1979. Dr. David J. Bruno, a Providence College graduate and past president of the Chiropractic Society of Rhode Island, has practiced here since 1979. Dr. Erika I. Bruno joined after earning her Doctor of Chiropractic degree in 2014.

Together with their chiropractic assistant, they offer patient-centered care for all ages, from children to seniors.
