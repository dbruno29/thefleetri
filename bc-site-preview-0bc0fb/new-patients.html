Your first visit

Your first visit to Bruno Chiropractic Center involves a few steps so that we can get to know you. Here is what to expect, along with our payment policies and the forms you can complete ahead of time.

What to expect

  1. Request an appointment

    Call (401) 353-5990 or send a request online. Same-day appointments are available for new patients.

  2. Complete your new patient forms

    Print and fill out the forms below before you arrive to save time at check-in. Bring your insurance card.

  3. Consultation and examination

    The doctor listens to your concerns and history, then examines you.

  4. Your treatment options

    The doctor explains what was found and recommends the treatment options that fit you, so you can decide with confidence.

Patient forms

Please print and fill out these forms so we can expedite your first visit.

New patient forms (PDF)

You will need a PDF reader such as Adobe Acrobat Reader to view or print the forms. Questions? Email brunochiroteam@gmail.com.

Being well prepared ensures the doctor has the information needed to provide the best possible care, and helps relieve any anxiety you may be feeling. Take a moment to review our staff page and get to know the doctors before you arrive.

Appointments, payment and insurance

Appointments

Bruno Chiropractic offers the flexibility of same-day appointments for new patients as well as current patients. Please call (401) 353-5990, email brunochiroteam@gmail.com, or request an appointment online.

Payment policies

Payment for all chiropractic services is due at the time of your appointment. We accept MasterCard, Visa, Discover, American Express, personal checks and cash.

Insurance and billing

We accept most traditional insurance plans. Please contact our office to verify acceptance of your plan. Qualifications for coverage may differ because each procedure is unique.

Common questions

Can I be seen today?

Often, yes. We offer same-day appointments for new and current patients. Call (401) 353-5990 and we will do our best to fit you in.

Do you accept my insurance?

We accept most traditional insurance plans and most major health plans. Call the office with your plan details and we will verify your coverage before your visit.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Your completed new patient forms and your insurance card. If you have recent imaging or reports related to your condition, bring those too.

What if I need help after hours?

In case of an emergency, call the regular office number, (401) 353-5990. A 24-hour answering service will take your call.

Is the office accessible?

Yes. For the ease of all our patients, Bruno Chiropractic is handicap accessible.

To provide low-cost, high-quality care to people of all ages, and to educate our patients so they can avoid similar problems in the future. Read more about our practice.
