Services

From sports injuries and carpal tunnel to back pain and headaches, Bruno Chiropractic is equipped to diagnose and treat your chiropractic symptoms. Here is what we offer and the conditions we see most.

Chiropractic adjustments

Adjustments restore motion to joints of the spine and extremities that have become restricted. The doctors choose the technique that suits you and your condition, from hands-on methods to the gentle, instrument-assisted Activator Method.

  • Activator Method: Dr. David Bruno is Advanced Activator Proficiency rated.
  • McKenzie Technique: Dr. Erika Bruno is certified for the full spine.

Supporting therapies

Alongside adjustments, we use therapies that calm inflammation and support healing between visits.

  • Ultrasound therapy for deep tissue warmth and circulation
  • Muscle stimulation to relieve spasm and pain
  • RockTape kinesiology taping: Dr. Erika Bruno is RockTape certified

Conditions we treat

If you don't see your concern here, call us. The doctors will tell you honestly whether chiropractic care is a good fit.

Back pain

Low back and mid-back pain are the most common reasons patients come to see us. A thorough exam helps us find the source and choose the right approach.

Neck and arm pain

Pain, stiffness or tingling that runs from the neck into the shoulder, arm or hand often traces back to the cervical spine. This includes carpal tunnel symptoms.

Headaches and migraines

Chronic headaches and migraines afflict many Americans. The severity can range from mild and intermittent to debilitating throbbing, unrelenting agony and nausea.

Whiplash

Whiplash is an injury to the cervical spine, or neck, and occurs when the muscles and other soft tissues are hyperextended or hyperflexed, most often in a rear-end collision.

Auto accident injuries

Even low-speed collisions can strain the neck and back, and symptoms sometimes appear days later. An early evaluation helps you recover sooner.

Sports injuries

Though there is no such thing as a “safe” sport, highly competitive sports such as football, weightlifting, gymnastics and wrestling pose particularly higher risks of injury, especially among children.

Injured workers

If you were hurt on the job, the doctors will evaluate your injury and work with you on a plan to get you back to work safely.

Personal injuries

Falls, lifting injuries and other everyday accidents can leave lasting pain. A thorough exam lets us treat the cause, not just the symptom.

Arthritis

Arthritis affects everyone in different ways. In some, joints in the spine, fingers, wrists, shoulders, knees, even toes, lose their normal shape and large amounts of fluid and debris fill the joint space.

Osteoporosis

Osteoporosis is a gradual disintegration of bone and it can have a devastating impact on the joints and vertebrae of your spine. Ask the doctors about gentle, low-force techniques such as the Activator Method.

Sleep apnea

Talk with the doctors about how chiropractic care may fit alongside the treatment plan from your physician or sleep specialist.
