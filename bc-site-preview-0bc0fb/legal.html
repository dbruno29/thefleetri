Disclaimer & patient privacy

Website disclaimer

The information on this website is provided for general educational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always seek the advice of your chiropractor, physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition. Never disregard professional advice or delay seeking it because of something you have read on this site.

Using this website or sending a message through it does not create a doctor–patient relationship. If you think you may have a medical emergency, call 911 immediately.

Patient privacy

Bruno Chiropractic Center respects your privacy. We collect only the information you choose to give us through this site, such as your name and phone number when you request an appointment, and we use it solely to respond to you and schedule your care. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing.

Please do not send Protected Health Information (PHI) through the forms on this website. Our full Notice of Privacy Practices, which describes how medical information about you may be used and disclosed under HIPAA, is available at the office. Ask any staff member for a copy, call (401) 353-5990, or email brunochiroteam@gmail.com.

Treatment photographs on this site are stock images of models and do not depict actual patients.
